# Week 4 – Learning Journey

### **Day 2**

The day began with a session on computational hardware, where we were introduced to the Arduino UNO and learned about its basic components, working principles, and applications. We explored how Arduino can be used to connect different hardware components and build simple interactive projects.

---

### **Day 3**

We worked on the Lucky Dangle project and also explored the concept of Chrome extensions. In the computational hardware session, we learned about sensors and actuators and understood how they are used to sense and respond to changes in the environment. We were also introduced to communication protocols such as UART, I2C, and SPI and learned about their basic working and applications. The session also covered the MPU6050 sensor, including its features and applications. We further studied the documentation of the SG90 servo motor to understand its specifications, working, and interfacing.

---

### **Day 4**

The session focused on the Internet of Things (IoT) and how different devices can communicate and exchange data through the internet. We explored the basic concepts and applications of IoT and how sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers can work together to create connected systems. We also had a session on “Off or Of,” which helped us explore the topic and understand its concepts in a practical way.
