Phase 01 Completed Week 02 of 20

Week 2 – Learning Journey

Exploring Emotional Branding, real-world problem identification, the Japanese 5S methodology, chatbot systems, algorithm fundamentals, UI design best practices, Scratch rapid creativity challenges, data analytics, and application prototyping using MIT App Inventor.

Day 1

Emotional Branding, Problem Identification & 5S Methodology

Branding & Workspace

The day started with a tech talk by Nandhini S on the Power of Emotional Branding, where we learned how emotions help brands create stronger connections with customers. We then worked on problem identification and learned how to identify real-world problems that could be solved through innovation.

Brahma conducted a session on the 5S Japanese methodology, which helped us understand the importance of maintaining an organised and efficient workspace. Later, we worked as the Red Cubical team and participated in team activities.

Day 2

Chatbots, Algorithms & Creative Problem Solving

AI & Logic

We started the day with a tech talk by Brindha on chatbots and their applications. Gowri then conducted a session on algorithms, where we learned how problems can be broken down into a set of logical steps.

We also participated in activities such as Podi Dosa, watched the video Think Like a Coder, and took part in a paper-and-paste activity. These activities helped us improve our creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Day 3

UI Design Principles & Rapid Scratch Challenges

UI/UX & Creativity

The day started with a tech talk by Jensy on designing user interfaces. We learned about the importance of creating simple, user-friendly, and visually appealing interfaces.

We then worked with Scratch and were given a challenge to create a meme within two minutes. This activity encouraged us to think quickly and use our creativity within a limited time.

Day 4

Data Analytics & MIT App Inventor Prototyping

Analytics & App Dev

Dakshana conducted a tech talk on data analytics, where we learned about the importance of analysing data and finding useful insights from it.

We also explored MIT App Inventor and learned the basics of developing applications using a simple and interactive platform. This session helped us understand how an idea can be developed into a functional application.
