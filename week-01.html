Week 1 – Learning Journey

Exploring social media algorithms, Design Thinking methodologies, data visualization with Power BI, behavioral decision frameworks, portfolio building in Antigravity IDE, and industry expert masterclasses.

Design Thinking & Analytics Cohort 2026
Day 1

Instagram Algorithms & Design Thinking

Tech Talk & Retail

We started the day with a Tech Talk by Vedika on how Instagram works and how its algorithm decides the type of reels shown to each user. It helped us understand how social media platforms analyse user interests and behaviour to personalise content.

Later, Dr. Meera conducted an insightful session on Design Thinking, where we learned about approaching problems creatively and understanding them from the user’s perspective.

In the afternoon, we were asked to choose a sector from the retail industry. Our team selected the Food and Beverage sector and prepared a presentation covering its development and evolution over the years.

Day 2

Power BI & SWOT Analysis

Analytics & Strategy

The day began with a Tech Talk by Affrina on Power BI. We learned how raw and unorganised data can be converted into meaningful and visually appealing dashboards using different charts and graphs, such as bar graphs. This gave us a basic understanding of how data can be analysed and presented effectively.

We also worked on SWOT Analysis, where we identified the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats of a given business or idea. Presenting our analysis helped us improve our teamwork, analytical thinking and communication skills.

Day 4

Prospect Theory & Antigravity Portfolios

Behavioral Economics & Tech

Parameshwari conducted a Tech Talk on Prospect Theory, which helped us understand how people make decisions when faced with risks, gains and losses. It gave us a different perspective on human decision-making and behaviour.

Later, we got a hands-on opportunity to create our own personal portfolios using Antigravity IDE. This allowed us to explore the platform while also presenting our skills, experiences and learning in a digital format.

Day 5

Base44, Goal Setting & Industry Masterclasses

Innovation & Industry

The day started with a Tech Talk by Lingaraj on Base44, where we learned about developing an application and explored how technology can be used to turn an idea into a functional product.

We then attended a session called “Goal, Vision and Glory” conducted by Ram, which encouraged us to think about our goals, future vision and what we aspire to achieve.

Later, we had an interactive session with our guest lecturers Sabareesh, Pranesh and Mounesh. Through our conversations with them, we gained valuable insights into the agriculture and textile industries and learned from their experiences and perspectives.
