# Week 2 – Learning Journey

### **Day 1**

The day started with a tech talk by Nandhini S on the Power of Emotional Branding, where we learned how emotions help brands create stronger connections with customers. We then worked on problem identification and learned how to identify real-world problems that could be solved through innovation. Brahma conducted a session on the 5S Japanese methodology, which helped us understand the importance of maintaining an organised and efficient workspace. Later, we worked as the Red Cubical team and participated in team activities.

---

### **Day 2**

We started the day with a tech talk by Brindha on chatbots and their applications. Gowri then conducted a session on algorithms, where we learned how problems can be broken down into a set of logical steps. We also participated in activities such as Podi Dosa, watched the video Think Like a Coder, and took part in a paper-and-paste activity. These activities helped us improve our creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

---

### **Day 3**

The day started with a tech talk by Jensy on designing user interfaces. We learned about the importance of creating simple, user-friendly, and visually appealing interfaces. We then worked with Scratch and were given a challenge to create a meme within two minutes. This activity encouraged us to think quickly and use our creativity within a limited time.

---

### **Day 4**

Dakshana conducted a tech talk on data analytics, where we learned about the importance of analysing data and finding useful insights from it. We also explored MIT App Inventor and learned the basics of developing applications using a simple and interactive platform. This session helped us understand how an idea can be developed into a functional application.
