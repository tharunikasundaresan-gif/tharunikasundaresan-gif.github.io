A Week of Learning, Connecting and Creating – My Protosem Journey

The first week of Protosem was an exciting blend of learning, teamwork, self-discovery and new experiences. From getting to know our peers to meeting inspiring entrepreneurs, every day brought something different and helped us step out of our comfort zones.

Open Dedicated Page ↗
Day 1

Breaking the Ice

Onboarding

We began our Protosem journey with an icebreaking session through a fun game of Stone Paper Scissors. It was a simple activity, but it gave us the opportunity to interact with almost everyone and helped us feel more comfortable with the group.

We then introduced ourselves and shared the things we were good at. This helped us understand each other's interests, talents and personalities. Later, we went on a Forge tour, where we got familiarised with the environment, facilities and the space where many of our upcoming activities would take place.

Day 2

Understanding Ourselves

Self-Discovery

The second day focused more on self-awareness. We took the 16 Personalities Test, which helped us understand our personality traits and characteristics better.

We were then asked to find a story on Zen Pencil that matched our personality. Each of us presented our chosen story and explained why we felt it reflected our character. It was interesting to see how the same activity could bring out different perspectives and interpretations from each person.

Day 3

Teamwork and Professional Growth

Team Dynamics

Day three was all about team building and collaboration. We formed our Beta teams and participated in activities such as Among Us and the Marshmallow Challenge. These activities encouraged us to communicate, think creatively and work together as a team.

We also had a LinkedIn session, where we learned how to create and develop our professional profiles. The session gave us an idea of how we can start building our professional identity and network even as students.

Day 4

Getting Ready for the Journey Ahead

Orientation & Launch

On the fourth day, we were once again familiarised with the rules and guidelines of Forge. We were also introduced to the leaderboard system and understood how it worked.

The highlight of the day was our launch event, where Mr. Kumar Rajagopalan, CEO of the Retailers Association of India, delivered an inspiring speech. His insights and experiences gave us a fresh perspective and motivated us to make the most of the opportunities provided through Protosem.

Day 5

Learning from Entrepreneurs

Entrepreneurship

The fifth day was an opportunity to learn directly from entrepreneurs. We had a Young Entrepreneurship Programme session with the founder of Thulasi Pharmacy, who shared his inspiring life journey, challenges and experiences as an entrepreneur.

We also interacted with the founder of A Toddler Thing, who is a graduate of our college. Listening to his entrepreneurial journey was especially inspiring because it showed us how someone from our own college community had turned an idea into a venture.

Later that afternoon, we presented our experiences from the first week of Protosem. We shared photographs, memorable moments and highlights from the different activities and sessions we had participated in throughout the week.

Retrospective

Looking Back at Week One

The first week of Protosem was much more than just a series of activities. It gave us opportunities to understand ourselves, connect with others, develop teamwork, build professional skills and learn from real-life entrepreneurs.

Every day brought a new lesson and a new experience. From the first icebreaking game to our final presentation, the week helped us become more confident, collaborative and open to new possibilities.

Week One was just the beginning, but it already gave us memories, connections and lessons that we can carry forward throughout our Protosem journey.
