# Week 5 – Learning Journey

### **Day 1**

The day started with a Tech Talk on the psychology of the human mind. We participated in a GEMS activity, where we explored how people make choices and how majority opinions can influence individual decisions. We also worked on website creation and explored the concept of comparing online prices with in-store pickup options.

---

### **Day 2**

We focused on problem identification and worked on creating a problem statement. We then conducted a survey to collect responses and understand the problem from different perspectives. This helped us connect the identified problem with real user experiences and requirements.

---

### **Day 3**

We worked on app creation using Figma and learned how to design and structure the different screens of an application. We also attended a Tech Talk on why AI hallucinates, where we explored the reasons behind AI-generated incorrect or misleading information and gained a better understanding of the limitations of AI systems.
