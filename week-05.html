Week 5 – Learning Journey

Exploring human psychology & GEMS decision dynamics, retail price comparison websites, rigorous problem identification and user survey validation, mobile UI app design in Figma, and understanding AI hallucinations and limitations.

Day 1

Psychology of Mind, GEMS Activity & Price Comparison Website

Cognition & Web Dev

The day started with a Tech Talk on the psychology of the human mind. We participated in a GEMS activity, where we explored how people make choices and how majority opinions can influence individual decisions.

We also worked on website creation and explored the concept of comparing online prices with in-store pickup options.

Human Psychology GEMS Activity Majority Bias & Choice Online vs In-Store Pricing Website Creation
Day 2

Problem Identification, Statement Framing & User Surveys

Research & Validation

We focused on problem identification and worked on creating a problem statement. We then conducted a survey to collect responses and understand the problem from different perspectives.

This helped us connect the identified problem with real user experiences and requirements.

Figma App Design & Tech Talk on AI Hallucinations

UI/UX & AI Limitations

We worked on app creation using Figma and learned how to design and structure the different screens of an application.

We also attended a Tech Talk on why AI hallucinates, where we explored the reasons behind AI-generated incorrect or misleading information and gained a better understanding of the limitations of AI systems.
